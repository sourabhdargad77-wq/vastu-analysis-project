Analysis Controls

V2 • Boundary + Polygon Center
Upload a plan → Trace Boundary → tap each outer corner in order → Finish Boundary. The app calculates the polygon centroid and locks the Chakra to it. You can still drag the center marker for fine adjustment. Choose where North lies on the uploaded plan (Up/Right/Down/Left), then enter the True-North bearing/offset in degrees. Degree rotates clockwise.
